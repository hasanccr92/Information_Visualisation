Visualization Guide

View Modes

🗺️
Map View: Geographic distribution. Good for regional patterns.
📊
Ranked View: Ordered bar list. Best for comparing magnitude of gaps.
🌍
Continental View: Abstract hexagon grid grouped by continent. Good for reducing clutter.

Interactions

👆
Select Countries: Click any country/bar/tile to add it to the comparison panel. Click again to remove.
⏳
Timeline: Drag the slider or press Play to see how the gender gap evolves from 2015 to 2025.
📈
Sparklines: Selected countries show a historical trendline (2015-2025) in the side panel.

Color Encoding

🎨
Blue = Male-favored gap (+)
Red = Female-favored gap (-)
White = Parity (~0)
