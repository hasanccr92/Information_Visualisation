Visualization Guide

Visual Encodings

📍
Position: Birth year (X) × Peak rating (Y)
⚫
Size: Player count (larger = more players)
🎨
Color: Gender (blue = male, red = female)
💧
Opacity: Rating homogeneity (opaque = similar skills)
📈
Trend Lines: LOESS smoothed patterns per gender
🗺️
Density Contours: Background heatmap showing concentration

Interactions

1. Timeline Animation: Click "Play" to animate through years (1950-2025).
2. Timeline Scrubbing: Drag the year slider for instant time navigation with ghost preview
3. Bubble Hover: Hover bubbles (Male or Female based on blue or red part of bubble) to see detailed stats and dashed connector lines to histograms.
4. Gender Toggle: Click legend items to show/hide genders.
5. Split View Mode: Click "Split View" to divide the canvas vertically (male left, female right) with gap chart in between.
6. Axis Zoom: Double-click on the chart to zoom to that region.
7. Histogram Filtering: Click marginal histogram bars (top/right) to filter by birth year or rating cohort. Multi-select supported for comparison.
8. Country Filter: Select a country from the dropdown to view country-specific patterns

Data Aggregation

Players are grouped into user input birth cohorts (default 5) and 50-point rating bins to prevent overplotting while preserving patterns.

Components

• Top Histogram: Birth year distribution
• Right Histogram: Rating distribution
• Gap Area Chart: Shows rating difference between genders (Split View only)
