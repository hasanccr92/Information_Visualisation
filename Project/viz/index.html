Exploring FIDE Chess Ratings

An interactive exploration of the FIDE chess ratings dataset, answering questions across three categories: global insights, subset analysis, and cross-subset comparisons. Dive into three distinct visualizations to uncover patterns in player performance, demographics, and representation.

📊
1

Birth Year vs Peak Rating

Explore the relationship between player birth year and peak chess rating achievement. This bubble chart reveals how different generations of players have achieved their highest ratings, with animated timeline scrubbing to observe rating progression over time.

Key Insight

"Peak ratings have risen dramatically over decades, with younger cohorts reaching higher ratings earlier in their careers."

Gender Representation Over Time

Track how female and male participation has evolved across different rating thresholds. Stacked area charts reveal the growth of the player pool and shifts in gender representation from beginners to elite levels.

Key Insight

"Female participation has grown significantly at lower rating tiers, but remains underrepresented at grandmaster levels."
